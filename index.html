How to play

Move the lure in front of a fish and click to cast. Wait for it to approach and bite, then click the Fish-On! circle at the reel to set the hook. Turn the yellow handle clockwise and keep the pressure gauge in the green at 50–65 RPM. The fish escapes after two complete revolutions below 40 RPM or above 85 RPM. Fish add 3–10 lb to your total weight. Press D to toggle the debug overlay.
